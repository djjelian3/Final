Our Story

Founded on one idea: your eyes deserve real attention.

iPrime Vision & Eyecare Specialists opened its doors in Davao City in 2014 with a single exam chair and a simple promise — every patient leaves seeing better than when they walked in.

Our Mission

Clarity, care, and a frame that actually fits you.

We believe eye care shouldn't feel rushed or transactional. Every exam at iPrime Vision includes time to actually talk — about how you use your eyes daily, what's changed, and what matters to you in a pair of glasses or lenses.

Today, we've grown into a full-service clinic with an in-house lab, a curated eyewear boutique, and a team of board-certified optometrists — but the exam chair philosophy hasn't changed.
